# News digest

When assembling a news digest for delivery:

- Lead with the single most consequential story.
- Group related headlines; never repeat the same story twice.
- Keep each item to one sentence plus its source.
- Close with a one-line "why it matters" for the lead item.
- Prefer plain, scannable phrasing over commentary.
